## 1. Production Notes & Acoustic Direction

All audio tracks are designed for natural, contemporary spoken English appropriate for **CEFR A1.2** learners (approximately **115–125 words per minute**).

- **Acoustic Signature:** Warm broadcast studio and realistic room tone without distracting background noise that obscures consonant clusters or auxiliary contractions (`isn’t`, `aren’t`, `I’m`).
- **Articulation Priority:** Clear enunciation of auxiliary verbs (`am`, `is`, `are`) and final `-ing` morphemes, while maintaining natural conversational prosody and intonation in `Yes/No` (rising intonation) and `Wh-` (falling intonation) questions.
- **Student Interface Integration:** Until the studio `.mp3` masters listed below are recorded and placed in `/public/assets/audio/`, the student interface explicitly identifies `eh-a12-pc-listen-01.mp3` as pending studio recording and provides a synchronized turn-by-turn transcript player with an opt-in Reference Voice Reader (never silently substituting studio audio).
