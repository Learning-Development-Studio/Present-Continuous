# DELIVERABLE 3 — TEACHER DOCUMENTATION (TEACHER’S PORTAL SPECIFICATION)

**Institution:** Learning & Development Studio  
**Platform:** English Hub  
**Experience Title:** RIGHT NOW: The World Is Happening  
**Target Level:** CEFR A1.2 (Subject to verification against the institutional Scope & Sequence)  
**Target Grammar System:** Present Continuous (Affirmative, Negative, Yes/No Questions, Wh- Questions, Short Answers, Spelling Patterns for `-ing`, Time Expressions)

> **Important Architectural Separation Notice:**  
> In compliance with Learning & Development Studio standards, none of the instructional notes, rubrics, facilitation guidance, or assessment criteria in this document appear in the student-facing interface. This specification is formatted for integration into the separate Teacher’s Portal.

---

## 1. Communicative Objectives

By exploring and completing *RIGHT NOW: The World Is Happening*, learners develop and demonstrate the ability to:

1. **Describe simultaneous ongoing actions:** Observe visual and written scenes and describe what individuals and groups are doing right now (`Elena is recording a podcast`, `Clara and Nadia are solving a problem`).
2. **Contrast expected vs. actual situations:** Use negative and affirmative Present Continuous statements to clarify what people are *not* doing versus what they *are* doing (`They aren’t taking a coffee break; they are testing a solution`).
3. **Inquire and exchange live information:** Formulate and respond to `Yes/No` questions (`Are they working inside an office today? — No, they aren’t.`) and `Wh-` questions (`What are you discussing today?`, `Why are they working at the same desk?`).
4. **Distinguish immediate actions from broader ongoing projects:** Use appropriate time expressions (`now`, `right now`, `at the moment` vs. `today`, `this week`) and distinguish ongoing actions from habitual routines where Simple Present is already established.

---

## 2. Prerequisite Competencies

To engage meaningfully with this experience without cognitive overload, learners should already possess:

- **Personal Subject Pronouns:** `I`, `you`, `he`, `she`, `it`, `we`, `they`, and basic singular/plural noun recognition.
- **Present Forms of `be`:** Recognition and use of `am`, `is`, `are` (affirmative, negative `isn’t / aren’t`, and basic inversion in `Yes/No` questions).
- **Foundational Question Words:** `What`, `Where`, `Who`, `Why`.
- **Simple Present Foundation (Optional Scaffolding):** Basic familiarity with Simple Present for routines (`Elena records podcasts on Tuesdays`) allows learners to appreciate the contrast in Module 03 Step 04.
- **Advanced Exclusions:** Future arrangements (`We are flying to Rome next Friday`) and complex stative verb exceptions are intentionally excluded at A1.2.

## 4. Grammar Focus

- **Affirmative Structure:** `Subject + am / is / are + verb-ing (+ complement + time expression)`
- **Negative Structure:** `Subject + am not / isn’t (is not) / aren’t (are not) + verb-ing`
- **Yes/No Questions & Short Answers:** `Am / Is / Are + subject + verb-ing?` → `Yes, I am. / No, she isn’t. / No, they aren’t.`
- **Wh- Questions:** `Wh- word + am / is / are + subject + verb-ing?`
- **Spelling Patterns for `-ing`:**
  1. **Pattern 1 (Regular):** `work → working`, `record → recording`, `carry → carrying`
  2. **Pattern 2 (Silent `-e`):** `prepare → preparing`, `solve → solving`, `share → sharing`, `organize → organizing`
  3. **Pattern 3 (1-Syllable CVC):** `sit → sitting`, `run → running`
- **Time Expressions:** `now`, `right now`, `at the moment`, `today`, `this week`

## 5. Learning Experience Overview & Pedagogical Progression

In accordance with Learning & Development Studio philosophy, **no mandatory lesson durations or rigid time limits are imposed**. Learners progress according to demonstrated understanding across one or multiple sessions:

1. **Immersive Opening (Hero):** Establishes the central inquiry (*"What’s happening around us right now?"*) through documentary photography of a live control room.
2. **Module 01 — Live Global Observation (Contextual Exposure & Reading):** Learners explore simultaneous dispatches from Madrid, Toronto, and London before seeing any grammar rules. They toggle the *Language Lens* to notice recurring structures and interpret incoming digital status messages.
3. **Module 02 — Active Lexicon Studio (Explicit Vocabulary):** Learners explore the 12 action phrases grouped by context, practice pronunciation and word stress, match visual camera clues to `-ing` phrases, and retrieve the forms in live sentences.
4. **Module 03 — Guided Grammar Investigation (Substantial Discovery):** Learners investigate authentic examples, manipulate the **Interactive Grammar Board** (testing Subject–Auxiliary agreement and switching between Affirmative, Negative, Yes/No, and Wh- forms), and complete the `-ing` Spelling Lab.
5. **Module 04 — Live Crossover & Multi-Modal Practice (Listening & Practice):** Learners follow a 3-speaker live crossover between Tokyo, Madrid, and London, complete detail and inference tasks, and work through three distinct practice stations (Sentence Reconstruction, Diagnostic Error Correction, and Schedule vs. Camera Info-Gap).
6. **Module 05 — Communicative Production & Final Challenge (*THE LIVE UPDATE*):** Learners produce original observations about their own environment, engage in a collaborative Partner A / Partner B information-gap exchange, and publish a 3-part live report based on a 4-zone simultaneous hub photograph.

---

## 6. Facilitation Guidance & Suggested Interaction Patterns

- **Individual Self-Paced Exploration (Module 01 & Module 02):** Allow learners to move freely between the three cities and inspect underlined phrases at their own pace. Encourage learners to read the contextual sentences aloud when using the pronunciation studio.
- **Guided Discovery Dialogue (Module 03):** While learners manipulate the Interactive Grammar Board, ask concept-checking questions rather than lecturing: *"What happens on the board when you change the subject from 'She' to 'They'?"* or *"Why does the board show a mismatch if we select 'She are'?"*
- **Pair Information-Gap Facilitation (Module 05, Part B):** Pair learners as **Partner A** and **Partner B**. Instruct Partner A to click `Partner A Screen` and Partner B to click `Partner B Screen`. Have them sit so they cannot see each other’s screens, requiring genuine oral question-and-answer exchanges to complete their logs.
- **Live Broadcast Showcase (Module 05, Final Challenge):** After learners draft their report in *The Live Update*, invite them to deliver their report orally as "live correspondents" while pointing to Zones A, B, C, and D on the main photograph.

### Holistic Assessment Criteria for *THE LIVE UPDATE*
- **Communicative Intelligibility (Primary):** Does the learner clearly communicate what different people in the hub are doing right now and how they respond to the camera battery alert?
- **Structural Control:** Does the learner pair subjects with appropriate auxiliaries (`am/is/are`) and `-ing` verbs across affirmative, negative, and question forms?
- **Lexical Appropriateness:** Does the learner integrate relevant action phrases and time expressions (`right now`, `at the moment`, `today`) naturally?

## 8. Extension Activities & Support Strategies

### Support Strategies (Scaffolding for Learners Requiring Additional Practice)
- **Auxiliary Color-Coding & Auto-Match:** Direct learners who omit auxiliaries (e.g., *"She recording"*) back to the **Interactive Grammar Board** in Module 03 to observe how the Auxiliary column (`am / is / are`) is mandatory in every sentence mode.
- **Zone Inspector Prompts:** In the Final Challenge, encourage learners to click each Zone card (Zones A–D) and use the *Useful Action Phrases* list before drafting full sentences.
- **Focused Form Practice:** Have learners focus first on singular third-person (`He / She is + verb-ing`) describing one person per zone before combining plural subjects (`Sofia and Liam are...`).

### Extension Activities (For Learners Ready for Deeper Challenge)
- **Live Window Observation:** Ask learners to look out a window or observe a busy common area and deliver a 60-second unscripted spoken commentary contrasting what people are doing today with what people usually do on weekends.
- **Follow-Up Interview Roleplay:** One learner plays a person in the Innovation Hub (Nina, Sofia, David, or Carmen) while another learner conducts a live 4-question interview using `Wh-` questions (`What are you working on this week?`, `Why are you testing that camera right now?`).
